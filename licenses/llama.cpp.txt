llama.cpp and ggml
------------------

llama.cpp (https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp) runs the model. In the app, the llama, ggml,
ggml-base, ggml-cpu-* and ggml-vulkan libraries next to the executable (and ggml-openvino in the
package for Intel NPUs) are built from it, vendored by llama-cpp-sys-2; in the browser extension,
it is compiled into wllama (see "wllama").
